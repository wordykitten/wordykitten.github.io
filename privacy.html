Privacy Policy

Wordy Kitten · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Wordy Kitten mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") uses and why. Wordy Kitten is made by an independent developer. If you have any question, write to wordykitten.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, items) is saved on your device and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud account. We never receive it.
  • To show ads, the game uses a trusted partner (Google AdMob). It may collect device information as described below.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (for example levels completed, stars, coins, owned cats and scarves, sound and reminder settings) on your device. It is also part of your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Google backup on Android).

On iPhone, if you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so you can continue on another device with the same Apple ID. This copy belongs to your iCloud account; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your device's Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

None of this information is sent to us. Deleting the game removes it from the device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are served through Google AdMob and its advertising partners. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, they may collect information such as your device type, operating system, IP address, approximate location (country or city level), the ads you see and tap, and an advertising identifier.

On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow tracking in Apple's "Allow apps to track" prompt. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you buy the "No Ads" pack, the game stops showing ad breaks and the banner; only the videos you choose to watch for a reward remain.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (with Google's consent window) before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Without consent, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings > Privacy choices.

Wherever you live, you can turn personalised ads off at any time in the game's Settings > Privacy choices ("Personalized ads"). Ads are still shown, but our ad partners are told not to use your information to personalise them and not to sell or share it for advertising (for example under US state privacy laws such as the CCPA).

Learn more in how Google uses information in ads and in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and the "No Ads" pack are sold through Apple's App Store (and Google Play on Android). The store processes the payment; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made, never your name, card or account details. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the game schedules friendly reminders (for example when your daily gift is ready) directly on your device. No data is sent anywhere to do this. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Children

Wordy Kitten is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses. Because we do not keep personal information ourselves, the easiest steps are: deleting the game (removes your saved progress), declining or turning off tracking in your device settings, and resetting your device's advertising identifier. For any request, email us and we will help, including with requests to our partners. You can also opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

Data security and retention

We do not run our own servers for the game. Information collected by our partners is kept and protected under their own policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
